Privacy Policy

How we handle information you share with us, whether that is through our valuation tool, an email, or a conversation.

Last updated: August 10, 2026

Salt Creek Advisory LLC (“Salt Creek,” “we,” “us,” or “our”) respects your privacy. This policy explains what information we collect through saltcreekadvisory.com (the “Site”), how we use it, and the choices you have. It applies to the Site itself, not to the confidential deal information we handle once a formal advisory engagement is underway, which is governed by the engagement letter and any separate non-disclosure agreement you sign with us.

Information We Collect

We collect information in three ways:

  • Information you submit directly. This includes your name, email address, phone number, company name, and any message content you provide through our email links or scheduling link.
  • Information you submit through the valuation tool. This includes industry and sub-sector selections, revenue and earnings ranges, growth trends, customer concentration, recurring revenue mix, owner involvement, and similar business characteristics you select to generate a directional estimate.
  • Automatically collected technical information. Like most websites, our hosting and infrastructure providers may log standard technical data such as IP address, browser type, device type, referring page, and timestamps, for security and site operation purposes.

This Site uses Google Analytics, a web analytics service provided by Google, to help us understand how visitors use the Site (for example, which pages are viewed and how visitors navigate the Site). Google Analytics uses cookies and similar technologies to collect this information, which may include your IP address; we use this data in aggregate and do not use it to identify you personally. We do not use analytics data for advertising or ad targeting, and we do not sell your information to third parties. You can opt out of Google Analytics tracking using the Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on or by adjusting your browser’s cookie settings. If we add other third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking tools, we will update this policy to describe what is used and why. Google may also collect information about your online activity over time and across other websites and services that use Google products. We do not control that collection. Google describes its practices at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

How We Use Information

We use the information above to:

  • Respond to your inquiries and schedule conversations;
  • Generate the directional estimate produced by our valuation tool;
  • Understand, at a general level, the businesses and owners reaching out to us, so we can prepare for a conversation;
  • Measure how the Site performs, including which pages, guides, and downloads visitors engage with, and which parts of the Site lead to inquiries;
  • Maintain, secure, and improve the Site; and
  • Comply with legal obligations, where applicable.

We handle everything you submit, including valuation tool inputs, as sensitive business information, and we limit who can access it. We do not use it for any purpose beyond the ones described here. Handling information carefully is not the same as a confidentiality agreement. As our Terms of Use explain, information you send through this Site is not covered by a confidentiality obligation until you and Salt Creek sign an NDA or an engagement letter, and we are glad to sign one before you send us anything sensitive.

How We Share Information

We do not sell your information, we do not share it for cross-context behavioral advertising, and we do not share it with third parties for their own marketing purposes. We use the service providers below to operate the Site. Each is bound to use your information only to provide its service to us:

  • Formspree, Inc. (United States) receives, delivers, and stores submissions from our valuation tool, including your name, email address, phone number, website, state, and the business details you enter. This is how your submission reaches us.
  • Cloudflare, Inc. (United States) hosts and delivers this Site and provides security and traffic analytics. Cloudflare processes technical data including your IP address, browser type, and the pages you request.
  • Google LLC (United States) provides Google Analytics, described above, and serves the web fonts used across the Site. Loading those fonts transmits your IP address to Google.
  • HelmIQ (United States), a separate company also owned by Salt Creek’s founder, Jack Pitts, provides the scheduling page reached through our “Schedule a Call” links. If you book a meeting, the name, email address, and any details you enter go to HelmIQ and to us.
  • Professional advisors (such as legal or accounting counsel), where necessary; and
  • Regulators or authorities, if required by law, subpoena, or other legal process.

If you engage Salt Creek formally as a client, information relevant to that engagement may be shared with prospective counterparties, lenders, or other transaction participants only with your knowledge and consent, consistent with standard M&A process practice and any confidentiality terms in your engagement letter.

Data Retention

We retain information you submit for as long as reasonably necessary to respond to your inquiry, maintain our business records, and comply with legal or professional obligations. You may request that we delete information you have submitted at any time, as described below.

Your Choices

You may:

  • Ask us what information we hold about you;
  • Ask us to correct or delete information you have submitted; and
  • Opt out of any future email communication from us at any time by replying and letting us know.

To make any of these requests, email us at jack@saltcreekadvisory.com or connor@saltcreekadvisory.com.

Do Not Track and Global Privacy Control

Some browsers can send a “Do Not Track” (DNT) signal or a Global Privacy Control (GPC) signal. There is no common industry standard for how a website should respond to a DNT signal, and this Site does not currently respond to DNT signals. We do not sell your personal information and we do not share it for cross-context behavioral advertising, so these signals do not change how we handle what you submit to us. You can still opt out of Google Analytics using the browser add-on linked above, or by blocking or clearing cookies in your browser settings.

Children’s Privacy

This Site is intended for business owners, investors, and professionals. It is not directed at, and we do not knowingly collect information from, children under the age of 13.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time, for example if we add new tools or third-party services to the Site. We will update the “Last updated” date above when we do. The August 2026 revision named the service providers we use to operate the Site, including Formspree, Cloudflare, Google, and HelmIQ, and added the section on Do Not Track and Global Privacy Control signals. Continued use of the Site after an update means you accept the revised policy.

Contact Us

Questions about this policy or how we handle your information can be directed to jack@saltcreekadvisory.com or connor@saltcreekadvisory.com.

We are not a chartered bank or depository institution, we do not accept deposits, and no funds held in connection with an engagement are FDIC or NCUA insured. We are not a broker-dealer registered with FINRA or the SEC and are not a registered investment adviser. To the extent applicable, our M&A engagements are conducted in reliance on the M&A broker exemption at Section 15(b)(13) of the Securities Exchange Act of 1934 (15 U.S.C. § 78o(b)(13)), under which “an M&A broker shall be exempt from registration” when acting solely in connection with the transfer of ownership of an “eligible privately held company,” defined as a company with no registered securities whose EBITDA is less than $25,000,000 or whose gross revenues are less than $250,000,000. That exemption is unavailable to any broker that holds client funds or securities, provides transaction financing, forms or represents a group of buyers, deals with a passive buyer, or represents both sides of a transaction without written disclosure and consent, and we do not engage in any of those activities. We hold or obtain state business broker licensure where required, and where a transaction calls for services only a licensed broker-dealer may perform, those services are referred to and executed through a properly registered broker-dealer. We do not hold, custody, or take possession of client funds or securities at any time. All engagements are advisory in nature; nothing on this site is an offer or solicitation to buy or sell any security, and nothing here is legal, tax, or investment advice.
