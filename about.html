About Salt Creek Advisory

Family-owned. Founder-led.
Built for owners like you.

Salt Creek Advisory is a family-owned, Chicago-based investment bank founded in 2026 by brothers Jack and Connor Pitts. We represent lower middle market owners who are selling their businesses, and only owners.

The principals

Jack and Connor grew up close, and starting Salt Creek Advisory together has been one of the better decisions either of them has made. They built this firm around the idea that owners deserve to work directly with the people actually doing the work. We didn’t build Salt Creek Advisory around clever positioning. Salt Creek Advisory LLC operates as an investment bank in the traditional lower middle market sense: we advise privately held business owners on the sale of their companies. We represent sellers only: we do not take buy-side engagements, we do not represent buyers, and we do not accept fees or other compensation from buyers. We are not a chartered bank or depository institution, we do not accept deposits, and nothing we offer is FDIC or NCUA insured. We are not a broker-dealer registered with FINRA or the SEC and are not a registered investment adviser. To the extent applicable, our M&A engagements are conducted in reliance on the federal M&A broker exemption at Section 15(b)(13) of the Securities Exchange Act of 1934 (15 U.S.C. § 78o(b)(13)), added by the Consolidated Appropriations Act, 2023 and effective March 29, 2023. Under that provision, “an M&A broker shall be exempt from registration” when it effects securities transactions solely in connection with the transfer of ownership of an “eligible privately held company” and reasonably believes the buyer will control the business and be active in its management afterward. An eligible privately held company is one with no class of securities registered with, or reporting to, the SEC that, in the fiscal year before the engagement, had EBITDA of less than $25,000,000 or gross revenues of less than $250,000,000 (amounts subject to periodic inflation adjustment). The exemption is not available to a broker that receives, holds, transmits, or has custody of the funds or securities being exchanged; acts for an issuer in a public offering; acts in a transaction involving a shell company; provides financing, or helps arrange third-party financing without complying with applicable law and disclosing its compensation in writing; represents both buyer and seller without clear written disclosure and the written consent of both; facilitates a transaction with a group of buyers it helped form; transfers ownership to a passive buyer; or binds a party to the transaction. Where a transaction would fall outside those conditions, we do not rely on the exemption, and any services that only a registered broker-dealer may perform would have to be provided by a registered broker-dealer, not by us. The federal exemption does not displace state law, and some states separately regulate business brokers and M&A intermediaries. We do not hold, custody, or take possession of client funds or securities at any time.
